Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-04-28. Contact: danielsdab2000@gmail.com.

Sonder is a real-time meeting copilot. This document describes exactly what Sonder collects, where it goes, and how to delete it.

What Sonder captures

Sonder captures audio from a meeting only when you click Start (in the macOS app or the Chrome side panel). A red pulsing indicator is visible at all times while audio is flowing. Stopping ends capture immediately.

By default Sonder runs entirely on your own machine: a local backend you operate, plus the macOS app or extension. Your audio never leaves your computer in the local-only mode.

When you opt into cloud transcription (by setting DEEPGRAM_API_KEY on your local backend), 16 kHz PCM audio is streamed over TLS to Deepgram for the duration of the session. When you opt into cloud answers (by setting CEREBRAS_API_KEY, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, etc.), the transcript text — not the audio — is sent to that LLM provider. Each provider has its own privacy policy; you control which providers Sonder talks to.

Sonder never writes raw audio frames to disk. PCM buffers are forwarded to STT and dropped from memory once transcribed.

Browser extension specifics

The Chrome extension uses chrome.storage.local only to cache the local server's auth token (read once at session start, never sent off-machine). It does not use cookies, IndexedDB, or analytics SDKs.

The extension only talks to http://127.0.0.1:8080 (your own local Sonder server). It does not connect to any remote service directly.

Consent

By clicking Start you affirm that you have the right to record this meeting under the laws applicable in your jurisdiction and the policies of the meeting platform. Sonder does not check this for you. See the consent notice for jurisdictional guidance and a suggested participant disclosure.

Your rights

  • Access: every file Sonder writes is in your home directory. Open it.
  • Deletion: delete the files listed above. There is no remote copy.
  • Portability: notes are stored as Apache Arrow (Lance); you can read them with any tool that speaks Lance.
  • Withdrawal: stop the server (pkill sonder-server) and uninstall the app/extension. No remote state is left behind.
