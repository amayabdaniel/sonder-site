Consent Notice

Read this once, then keep it where your participants can find it.

Sonder listens to meeting audio you have explicitly authorized it to capture. It is not a hidden recorder. The product is designed around four rules:

  1. You start it. Sonder never auto-joins or auto-records. Capture begins only after you click Start (or trigger the keyboard shortcut), and ends the moment you click Stop, close the app, or close the tab.
  2. It is visible. Whenever audio is being captured, Sonder shows a pulsing red indicator in the macOS menu bar AND in the Chrome side panel. The indicator never lies — if it's red, audio is flowing.
  3. You own the data. Transcripts, notes, and cached answers live in ~/.sonder/ on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded except (a) audio you opt into sending to a cloud STT provider, and (b) transcript text you opt into sending to a cloud LLM provider. Both are off by default.
  4. You can leave clean. Stop the server, uninstall the app/extension, delete ~/.sonder/, and there is no trace.

Suggested participant disclosure

Display this once at the start of any meeting where Sonder is active:

Heads up — I'm using a real-time AI copilot during this meeting. It transcribes the audio locally and helps me draft notes and answer questions. Please let me know if you'd prefer I disable it.

Jurisdictional notice

Recording-consent law varies by country and US state. Sonder does not check whether you have the right to record. It is your responsibility to know:

  • Two-party / all-party consent states (US): California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Montana, Nevada, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania, Washington — explicit consent from every participant is typically required.
  • GDPR / European Economic Area: processing meeting audio is "personal data." A lawful basis (contract, legitimate interests, consent) is required; for sensitive content, explicit consent.
  • Workplace policies: your employer's policy may forbid third-party copilots regardless of legality. Check before you ship a recording.

When in doubt: ask first, or don't use Sonder for that meeting.

What to tell anyone who asks

  • "What's the red dot?" — "It's the indicator that my AI copilot is listening to this meeting. I started it; it's local; I can stop it; I'd love to share what it generates if you want."
  • "Where does the audio go?" — "If I have cloud transcription on, audio goes over TLS to my STT provider for the call duration only. If I'm in local mode, it doesn't leave my laptop."
  • "Where are the transcripts?" — "On my disk in ~/.sonder/. I can hand you a copy or delete them on demand."
